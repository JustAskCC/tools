About us

The city is more than a place. It’s the people.

The University Heights City Council authorized the creation of the University Heights City Beautiful Corporation, the city’s Community Improvement Corporation (CIC). We work alongside the City of University Heights to advance its industrial, economic, commercial, and civic development and to help carry out the City of University Heights Master Plan.

Our focus is the families, children, and seniors who call University Heights home, and the local businesses that serve them. We keep our programs right here in University Heights, close to the people they are meant for.

Connect with the CIC

Our Official Purpose

Authorized by the University Heights City Council for the “purpose of advancing, encouraging and promoting the industrial, economic, commercial and civic development of the City of University Heights,” and for the purpose of implementing the City of University Heights Master Plan.

Community programs

There’s a place for you here.

Programs built around what University Heights families, children, seniors, and small businesses need most.

Home Repair Workshops

Free, hands on classes for University Heights residents with our partner, the Home Repair Resource Center: Power Tools 101, Carpentry and Drywall, Electrical Switches and Outlets, and Plumbing Repair Basics. Each class holds up to 20 people.

See upcoming classes

Help for Seniors

Helping older neighbors age in place safely. We are working with the Home Repair Resource Center on home repair grants for seniors, plus benches and other comforts around town.

Request information

Neighbor Mediation

A neighbor dispute over a fence, a tree, or noise? The CIC covers mediation sessions with the Cleveland Mediation Center so residents can work things out.

Ask about mediation

Fresh Food Access

A monthly fresh produce program that brings healthy, affordable food closer to University Heights families.

A look at what we have done, what is underway, and what is coming next for University Heights, and where CIC funds are going.

Completed

Home Repair Resource Center partnership

Signed an agreement with the Home Repair Resource Center to bring free home repair classes to University Heights residents.

Workshops

Carpentry and Drywall workshop

Residents learned hands on carpentry and drywall repair in our first workshop with the Home Repair Resource Center.

Residents

Approved a contract with the Cleveland Mediation Center so the CIC can cover mediation sessions for residents.

In Progress
