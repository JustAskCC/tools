About Talk It Out

  • The Cool Kids help children name feelings, think through friend problems, and work through homework step by step. They guide instead of giving answers.
  • They always point children to the trusted adults in their life. If a child mentions being hurt, unsafe, or wanting to hurt themselves, a help screen opens with steps to find an adult, plus 988 and 911.
  • Chats are not saved on the device or the server. Only the child's first name, age, and favorite Cool Kid are remembered on this device, and the name never leaves the device.
  • Voices are free and run on this device. The first time, about 90MB downloads, so WiFi is best.
  • The Cool Kids are an AI helper, not a counselor or therapist. Please use Talk It Out alongside a caring adult.
