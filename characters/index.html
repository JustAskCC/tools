Chloe

The big sister and the emotional lead of the universe.

Age 11Sixth gradeBig sister

Stylish, witty and observant. Confident on the outside, sensitive on the inside. She reads a room before the adults do and is learning that confidence is not pretending everything is fine.

“I noticed it before anybody said it out loud.”

Teaches kids: friendship, confidence, leadership and how to name big feelings.

Age 9Fourth gradeChess masterMusic producer in training

Why it matters

Every kid should see themselves in the story.

13%

of 2025 children’s books were about Black people, down from 15.8% the year before.

Mostly

white, male and able bodied: the characters in best selling preschool storybook apps.

47%

of parents say Black representation in kids’ media is often stereotypical.

Kids engage more with stories that reflect them. Cool Kids University puts every background, body type, personality and family in the story, so every child can see who they are and who they can become. Learn. Wonder. Grow.

Sources: Cooperative Children’s Book Center 2025; Journal of Children and Media 2025; Common Sense Media.
