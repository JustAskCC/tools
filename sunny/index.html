For grown ups

Sunny is an AI talk buddy for kids. It helps kids name feelings, think through friend problems, and work through homework step by step (it guides instead of giving answers). It always encourages kids to talk with a trusted adult, and if a child mentions being hurt, unsafe, or wanting to hurt themselves, Sunny shows a help card telling them to find a grown up right away, with the 988 Suicide and Crisis Lifeline (call or text 988) and 911 for emergencies.

Conversations are not saved on the phone or the server. Starting over or closing the app clears everything.

Sunny is not a therapist and is not a replacement for a parent, teacher, or counselor. Please use it alongside a caring adult.
