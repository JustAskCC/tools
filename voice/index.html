Voice Studio

Turn any script into natural narration. Pick a voice, choose Narrate, Teach or Storytime, and download the audio. Free voices run right on your device, with no credits and no account.

1Choose your voices

The first time, your device downloads the voice model (about 90MB). After that it's saved, so it opens quickly next time. Use WiFi for the first load.

Paste your ElevenLabs API key once. It stays on this device and is sent only to ElevenLabs. Get a key at elevenlabs.io, Settings, API Keys.

2Pick a voice

Tap a voice to choose it. Tap Sample to hear it.

3Pick a reading style

Each style sets the pace and the pauses between paragraphs. You can fine tune below.

Speed
Pause between paragraphs
Expression
More settings
Consistency
Higher sounds steadier. Lower sounds more emotional.

4Write or paste your script

Long scripts are fine. Leave a blank line between paragraphs for a natural pause. Punctuation matters: commas and periods tell the voice where to breathe.
